Mission & Scientific Objectives

DEC. 2026 ROADMAP

The global electric power grid is undergoing a generational paradigm shift from centralized synchronous generation to distributed, stochastic, inverter-dominated architectures. Large-scale penetration of solar photovoltaics, offshore wind turbines, and utility battery storage introduces unprecedented challenges in low-inertia frequency stability, transient voltage regulation, and locational market volatility.

100RE Laboratory at Hanoi University of Science and Technology directly addresses these critical energy transition challenges through high-fidelity mathematical optimization, digital twin modeling, and physical hardware testbeds. Our research outputs support national energy planners, system operators, and international academia in realizing a stable 100% renewable power grid.

PILLAR 01 · OPTIMIZATION & MARKETS

100% RE Transition Pathways & Market Dispatch

Multi-period generator scheduling under extreme renewable stochasticity. Formulating Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) models, dynamic nodal pricing (LMP), and flexibility compensation for distributed energy resources.

Objective: Min Σ [ C_gen(P_it) + C_startup(u_it) + C_curtail(w_it) ]
Subject to: Real-time Power Balance, Ramp Rates, N-1 Branch Security Limits
Team Lead: Nguyen Tuan Anh Research Details →
PILLAR 02 · SOLAR TESTBED

Solar Photovoltaics & SCADA

Dynamic PV array matrix reconfiguration to mitigate partial shading losses. Rooftop solar hosting capacity evaluation on HUST distribution feeders.

P_mpp = f(G_irr, T_cell)
Array Switching Matrix: Active
Lead: Bui Quang Minh Explore →
PILLAR 03 · WIND INTEGRATION

Wind Aerodynamics & LVRT

Wake effect mitigation, Low Voltage Ride Through (LVRT) compliance, and synthetic inertia emulation for offshore and onshore wind turbines.

V_sag ≥ 0.15 p.u. (LVRT Ride-through)
Power Smoothing via Wind-BESS
Lead: Nguyen Hoang Nam Explore →
PILLAR 04 · ENERGY STORAGE

BESS & Cell Degradation

Electrochemical SoC/SoH state estimation, multi-temperature battery thermal management, and rapid frequency response (FFR) control.

SoC_t+1 = SoC_t - (η · I_t / C_nom)
FFR Response: < 200 ms latency
Lead: Nguyen Quang Anh Explore →
PILLAR 05 · SECTOR COUPLING

Green Hydrogen & Fuel Cells

Renewable-powered PEM water electrolysis, seasonal chemical storage buffers, and deep decarbonization pathways for industrial heating and transport.

2 H2O ➔ 2 H2 + O2 (ΔH = 285.8 kJ/mol)
Electrolyzer Efficiency: η ≥ 74%
Lead: Nguyen Hoang Anh Explore →
PILLAR 06 · INTELLIGENT SYSTEMS

Smart Grid Automation, Microgrids & Edge AI

Decentralized multi-agent control for isolated microgrids, Virtual Power Plants (VPP), and deep learning SAM-LSTM neural networks for high-precision renewable forecasting and peer-to-peer trading.

VPP Dispatch: P_vpp = Σ P_pv + Σ P_wind ± Σ P_bess − Σ P_dr
SAM-LSTM 15-min Ahead Forecasting RMSE < 3.2%

Laboratory Leadership

HEAD OF LABORATORY

Assoc. Prof. Nguyen Duc Tuyen, Ph.D.

Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST) · Shibaura Institute of Technology (SIT)

Assoc. Prof. Nguyen Duc Tuyen received his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Shibaura Institute of Technology (Tokyo, Japan). He founded the 100RE Laboratory in July 2018. His research focuses on high-penetration renewable energy integration, smart grids, dynamic energy management systems, battery storage optimization, and electricity markets. He has authored over 200 scholarly papers in peer-reviewed journals and international conferences.
