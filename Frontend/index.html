What are our Goals?

The power grid is undergoing a profound structural transformation fueled by the rapid growth of renewable energy sources such as solar photovoltaics, wind power, and battery storage. The traditional centralized, one-directional grid architecture is evolving into a deregulated, multi-dimensional ecosystem characterized by multi-layered data streams, intelligent edge controllers, and flexible demand response mechanisms.

While smart cities, microgrids, and electric transportation present tremendous opportunities for decarbonization, they introduce critical challenges regarding frequency stability, voltage regulation, power quality, and cybersecurity. Many countries and international organizations are ambitiously advancing toward 100% renewable energy penetration.

100RE Laboratory was founded to directly address these complex technical and economic questions through rigorous mathematical modeling, real-time hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) simulations, artificial intelligence forecasting, and field experimental validations in Vietnam and internationally.

Assoc. Prof. Nguyen Duc Tuyen, Head of 100RE Laboratory
July 05, 2018

Welcome to the 100RE Research Platform

The 100% Renewable Energy Research Platform (100RE Lab) is part of the Department of Power Systems, School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST). The Research Platform conducts scientific inquiry and application-oriented engineering in power system stability, smart grid automation, renewable energy integration, energy storage, green hydrogen, and computational intelligence. 100RE Lab oversees the scientific operation and continuous advancement of university-grade experimental infrastructures, microgrid testbeds, and collaborative international research platforms.

100RE Energy Lab Infrastructure

The 100RE Laboratory is a comprehensive academic research infrastructure in which the dynamic interactions of renewable generation components are investigated and novel grid-stabilization algorithms are tested under realistic conditions. A synchronized network of installations links rooftop solar photovoltaic arrays, wind power simulators, battery energy storage systems (BESS), green hydrogen electrolyzers, and electric vehicle (EV) charging facilities through high-speed digital SCADA and industrial communication protocols. The overarching objective of our scientific work is to optimize the transport, distribution, storage, and intelligent dispatch of clean electricity, thereby establishing the technological foundation for Vietnam's Net-Zero energy transition.

Control, Monitoring & Smart Grid Automation Center

Research control room of the Mini-SCADA Testbed in the 100RE Energy Lab (Photo: 100RE Laboratory, HUST)

The Control, Monitoring & Smart Grid Automation Center is a research-oriented infrastructure designed for modeling, developing, and validating next-generation supervisory control and automation architectures for resilient power distribution networks. Leveraging dedicated industrial SCADA workstations, Modbus/DNP3 industrial communication protocols, and high-speed data acquisition hardware, the center delivers real-time telemetry, automated fault isolation, adaptive voltage regulation, and decentralized microgrid dispatch.

The laboratory interfaces physical renewable generation assets with simulated utility feeders, allowing researchers to evaluate active distribution network dynamics, Virtual Power Plant (VPP) coordination, and cybersecurity protocols under high renewable penetration.

Solar Energy & Photovoltaic Systems Laboratory

Rooftop Solar PV SCADA Experimental Array at HUST D9 Building (Photo: 100RE Laboratory, HUST)

The Solar Energy & Photovoltaic Systems Laboratory conducts rigorous experimental and analytical research on photovoltaic cell modeling, high-efficiency Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT), and dynamic solar irradiance forecasting. The laboratory operates a dedicated rooftop PV research station on the D9 building at HUST, paired with precision multi-channel pyranometers and weather monitoring instruments.

A core research focus is the mitigation of partial shading losses through dynamic array reconfiguration using solid-state switching matrices. In addition, the laboratory models grid-hosting capacity constraints, power electronic inverter harmonics, and low-voltage ride-through (LVRT) performance for urban commercial and industrial solar rooftops across Vietnam.

PV Team • Leader: Bui Quang Minh Explore Team & Projects

Wind Energy Dynamics & Grid Integration Laboratory

Wind Power Generation Simulation & Dynamic Grid Stability Platform (Source: 100RE Laboratory)

The Wind Energy Dynamics & Grid Integration Laboratory provides advanced computational modeling and experimental validation of wind turbine aerodynamics, power conversion systems (PCS), and multi-turbine wake interactions. Research investigates both onshore wind farms in complex terrain and offshore floating wind installations in Vietnam's central coastal waters.

Specialized test algorithms focus on enhancing Low Voltage Ride Through (LVRT) capabilities during severe transmission voltage dips, synthetic inertia emulation using doubly-fed induction generators (DFIG) and permanent magnet synchronous generators (PMSG), and hybrid wind-storage smoothing schemes to curb ramp-rate violations.

Wind Team • Leader: Nguyen Hoang Nam Explore Team & Projects

Battery Energy Storage Systems & Frequency Control Laboratory

Grid-Connected Battery Energy Storage (BESS) Regulation Bench (Photo: 100RE Laboratory, HUST)

The Battery Energy Storage Systems & Frequency Control Laboratory investigates utility-scale and distributed electrochemical energy storage for transmission grid support and microgrid autonomy. The laboratory develops real-time algorithms for primary frequency regulation (PJM RegD standard), synthetic inertia injection, and dynamic voltage stabilization.

Through advanced battery cycling data and electro-thermal modeling, researchers formulate multi-objective dispatch strategies that optimize energy arbitrage and peak shaving while rigorously minimizing lithium-ion degradation (capacity fade and internal resistance growth). The team actively consults for national organizations (VIETSE, GIZ) on national BESS deployment roadmaps.

BESS Team • Leader: Nguyen Quang Anh Explore Team & Projects

Green Hydrogen & Power-to-Gas Research Center

Solar-Coupled Water Electrolyzer & PEM Fuel Cell Test Platform (Photo: 100RE Lab & AIST Japan)

The Green Hydrogen & Power-to-Gas Research Center focuses on the decarbonization of heavy industry and long-duration seasonal storage through renewable-driven hydrogen synthesis. Research encompasses alkaline and proton exchange membrane (PEM) water electrolyzer modeling, direct coupling of fluctuating solar/wind power with electrolysis stacks, and fuel cell micro-cogeneration (CHP).

Drawing on collaborative research with Japanese institutions (AIST, Shibaura Institute of Technology), the center analyzes techno-economic supply chain feasibility, hydrogen storage logistics, and ammonia co-firing pathways to empower Vietnam's marine transport and industrial hubs toward net-zero emissions.

Hydrogen Team • Leader: Nguyen Hoang Anh Explore Team & Projects

Electric Vehicle Smart Charging & V2G Laboratory

Bidirectional EV Charging Station & V2G Power Electronics Testbed (Photo: 100RE Laboratory)

The Electric Vehicle Smart Charging & V2G Laboratory examines the deep technical interplay between electrified transport fleets and power distribution networks. Research models the spatial-temporal charging behavior of electric cars, electric bus fleets (e.g. VinBus in Hanoi), and commercial logistics, quantifying transformer overloading and harmonic distortion.

The laboratory develops bidirectional Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) and Vehicle-to-Building (V2B) power electronic interfaces, smart decentralized charging controllers, and dynamic tariff response systems that turn parked EV fleets into flexible grid-stabilizing assets while safeguarding battery state-of-health.

EV Team • Leader: Dao Quoc Khanh Explore Team & Projects

Artificial Intelligence & Deep Learning Energy Analytics Center

High-Performance GPU Computing Cluster for Renewable AI Forecasting (Photo: 100RE Laboratory)

The Artificial Intelligence & Deep Learning Energy Analytics Center harnesses data-driven paradigms, deep neural networks, and distributed ledger technologies to solve high-dimensional optimization challenges in modern power engineering. Key algorithmic breakthroughs include hybrid Spatial Attention Mechanism - Long Short-Term Memory (SAM-LSTM) architectures and Evolutionary Deep Self-Attention networks for ultra-short-term solar irradiance and wind power forecasting.

The center also pioneers computer-vision-based automated fault detection on aerial solar PV arrays, physics-informed neural networks (PINN) for dynamic line rating, and decentralized blockchain smart contracts for peer-to-peer (P2P) renewable energy trading within urban microgrids.

AI Team • Leader: Nguyen Trong Thanh Explore Team & Projects

Unit Commitment, Economic Dispatch & Demand Response Laboratory

Stochastic Unit Commitment & Demand Response Optimization Cluster (Source: 100RE Laboratory)

The Unit Commitment, Economic Dispatch & Demand Response Laboratory advances mathematical optimization models to ensure economic, security-constrained generator scheduling in power grids with high renewable variability. Using Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP), Genetic Algorithms (GA), and stochastic rolling-horizon formulations, the laboratory solves large-scale unit commitment incorporating spinning reserves and transmission line limits.

On the consumption side, the laboratory designs incentive-based and critical-peak pricing Demand Response (DR) mechanisms. Dynamic load-shifting models quantify consumer flexibility and comfort elasticity, allowing commercial buildings and industrial loads to actively balance the grid and minimize system operating expenditures.
