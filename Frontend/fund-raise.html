Support & Fund Raise

Join hands with 100RE Laboratory in our 20-year vision to build a world-class clean energy research laboratory, fostering top-tier engineering talent in Vietnam.

Our 20-Year Vision & Funding Priorities

Founded in July 2018 at Hanoi University of Science and Technology, 100RE Lab aims to become a leading international research hub for smart grid technologies, hydrogen integration, and power system decarbonization. To achieve this ambitious vision, we actively welcome collaboration and support in the following areas:

1. Experimental Apparatus & Licenses

Donation of power system measurement instruments, oscilloscopes, fuel cell test kits, or professional simulation licenses (PSCAD, DigSILENT, HOMER Pro).

2. Academic Mentors & Consultants

Experienced researchers and industry experts willing to advise students, co-author publications, and give guest lectures.

3. Overseas Graduate Admissions

Connecting outstanding 100RE Lab graduates with Master/PhD scholarship opportunities at top international research universities.

4. Student Recruitment

Corporate partnerships offering internships and fast-track recruitment for our trained power system and renewable energy engineers.

5. Computing & Office Hardware

High-performance GPU workstations for AI model training, monitors, and laboratory office infrastructure.

6. Specialized Training Programs

Sponsoring training workshops on renewable energy standards, power market dispatching, and hydrogen safety.

7. Financial & Publication Grants

Financial aid covering open-access journal publication fees (IEEE, Elsevier), international conference travel, and student stipends.
