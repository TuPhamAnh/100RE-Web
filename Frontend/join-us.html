How to be One of Us?

Are you passionate about renewable energy, smart power systems, AI, and green hydrogen? Here is how to apply and become a member of 100RE Laboratory.

Eligibility & Requirements

  • Strong Passion for Clean Energy: Genuine interest in power systems, solar PV, wind, hydrogen, AI, or battery storage.
  • International Ambition: Desire to pursue graduate studies abroad (Master/PhD) or work for international technology corporations.
  • English Proficiency: Capable of reading academic publications, writing technical reports, and presenting research.
  • Good Academic Performance: Solid background in electrical engineering, mathematics, computer science, or energy engineering.
  • Dedication & Positive Attitude: Hardworking, responsible, proactive, and committed to collaborative teamwork.

Application Checklist

Please prepare the following documents and submit them via email:

  1. Curriculum Vitae (CV): Detailing your academic background, GPA, programming skills, and any past projects.
  2. Letter of Interest (Cover Letter): Explaining:
    • Brief self-introduction and personal research interests;
    • What you know about 100RE Laboratory;
    • Why you want to join 100RE Lab and your chosen research team;
    • Your short-term and long-term academic/career orientation.
Send your application package directly to: tuyen.nguyenduc@hust.edu.vn
