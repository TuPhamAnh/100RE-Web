### Design Specificity Verdict
- **LLM Assessment**: The platform has undeniable authentic academic authority in renewable energy engineering (HIL testbeds, microgrid SCADA, IEEE manuscripts, 8-9 research teams). However, it suffers from a split identity between a 2000s-style Wix public shell and a modern SciNote SaaS workspace.
- **Deterministic Scan**: 110+ findings across 57 files. 42 side-tab accent stripes (`border-left/top: 4px solid #16a34a`), 12 low-contrast color violations (`#81a2b1` at 2.7:1), 18 overused font declarations (`Inter` monoculture), 9 undersized text instances (<11px), 1 empty image tag (`photos.html:115`), and 800+ lines of inline style debt.

### Priority Issues
1. **[P0] Low Contrast & Accessibility Violations**: Banner background `#81a2b1` gives 2.7:1 contrast on white text; touch targets are below 44px on mobile buttons.
2. **[P1] Side-Tab Accent Border Overuse (AI Slop Tell)**: 42 cards and containers use 3px–5px solid green/yellow accent stripes on a single side.
3. **[P1] Visual Identity Fracturing & Split Design System**: Public site uses square corners (`0px`) and Wix red (`#df3336`); Workspace uses rounded tokens and emerald green.
4. **[P2] Navigation Cognitive Overload**: 20 simultaneous links in the public header overwhelm users and violate Cowan's <=4 working memory limit.
5. **[P2] Fragile Native Browser prompt() for Supervisor Sign-offs**: Lab approvals rely on unstyled `window.prompt()`.

### Persona Red Flags
- **Alex (Power User)**: No keyboard accelerators (`Cmd+K`, `N`); no batch operations.
- **Jordan (First Timer)**: Overloaded navigation; confusion between 9 public research areas vs 8 workspace teams.
- **Sam (Accessibility)**: Low contrast on `#81a2b1` elements; touch targets < 44px; sub-11px text.
- **Riley (Stress Tester)**: Dataset uploads lack client-side MIME/size validation.
